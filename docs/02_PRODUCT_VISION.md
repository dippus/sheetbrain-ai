# 02 — Product Vision & Strategic Positioning

> **Document Status**: Approved  
> **Alignment**: Workplace Efficiency Track

---

## 1. The Core Industry Problem

Over **1.2 Billion professionals** use spreadsheets daily. However:
1. **Financial Modeling is Repetitive & Error-Prone**: 88% of corporate spreadsheets contain human formula errors.
2. **Generative AI is Disconnected From Structured Data**: When asked for a financial projection, ChatGPT or Claude outputs a dead markdown table in text. You cannot click cells, formulas are non-existent, and modifying one number does not recalculate the model.
3. **Existing Office Copilots are Expensive & Rigid**: Solutions like Microsoft 365 Copilot ($30/user/month) require enterprise suite lock-in and do not offer autonomous multi-agent scenario simulations.

---

## 2. The Vision: The Autonomous Office Canvas

SheetBrain AI treats the spreadsheet not as a file, but as a **living computational canvas for AI agents**:
* The human specifies the business intent in plain language.
* Specialized AI agents act as the **Data Architect**, **Financial Modeler**, and **BI Analyst**.
* The user interacts with an industrial-grade spreadsheet powered by **Univer SDK**, modifying cells and testing hypotheses with complete transparency.

---

## 3. Target Personas

### Persona A: The Startup Founder & Operator
* **Need**: Rapidly assemble a 12-to-18 month cash runway, burn rate, and hiring budget for investors.
* **Pain**: Spends 6+ hours tweaking Excel templates from scratch.

### Persona B: The Growth & Marketing Lead
* **Need**: Model CAC, LTV, channel payback periods, and ROAS across multiple paid ad channels.
* **Pain**: Formulas break when new marketing channels or rows are added.

### Persona C: The Financial & Operations Analyst
* **Need**: Perform quarterly budget variance and sensitivity modeling.
* **Pain**: Running manual "what-if" scenarios requires duplicating entire worksheets.
